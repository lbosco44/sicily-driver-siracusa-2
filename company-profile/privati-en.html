Who we are

We don't offer simple transfers.
We create experiences.

Sicily Driver Siracusa is a young, dynamic company born from a passion for high-quality private hire and transfers. From Syracuse we accompany travellers, couples, hotel guests and families across the roads of eastern Sicily.

Luxury vans — including the Mercedes V-Class, GLB Premium and E-Class — cared for as if they were our own, drivers who speak Italian and English (real English, not Google Translate), service around the clock. No promises of "excellence": you see it in the journey.
